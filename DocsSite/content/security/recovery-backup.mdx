Some accounts Wrenai trades from are created and held by Wrenai: the trading account for native NEAR swaps, and the Hyperliquid addresses for Perps. A **recovery backup** gives you your own copy of their keys, so you can reach those funds without the app.

You must save one before funding these accounts.

## Save a backup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Go to **Settings**, then **Recovery**. Choose the account to back up if you have agents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a password">
    Enter a password of at least 12 characters, twice. It never leaves your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve and download">
    Press **Save backup**. Your wallet signs a message to prove you own the account, then the file is encrypted in your browser and downloaded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Tick that you saved the file and its password, then press **Confirm backup**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The file is encrypted on your device with your password (PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations, then AES-GCM). Wrenai never sees the password.

<Callout tone="warning">
  Keep the file and the password in separate safe places. Wrenai can't recover a lost password, and the confirmation can't prove you kept the file.
</Callout>

## Recover without the app

Keep a copy of the Wrenai project. From its app folder, run:

```bash
pnpm recover backup.json recovered.key
```

Add an agent's index to recover its separate Perps address. The command asks for your password, works offline and writes the private keys to a new file only you can read.

## After a new backup

If Wrenai adds a new kind of account, Settings shows **Update needed**. Download a new backup before funding that account; your old backup stays valid for the accounts it covers.
