**Memecoins** lists NEAR meme tokens from Nearly and Meme.Cooking, with their market figures and a risk grade, so you can look before you trade.

## What the table shows

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Price change | The move over the selected period |
| Liquidity | How much is in the token's pool |
| Market cap | Supply times price |
| Volume | Trading over the last 24 hours |
| Holders | How many accounts hold it |
| Grade | The risk grade: Clean, Caution, Danger or Not graded |

Search by name, filter by launchpad, or sort by gainers.

## Reading the grade

The grade summarizes the token's risk report from Squint. **Not graded** means the report isn't available, and Wrenai treats that as unknown, not as safe.

Meme Hunter is stricter than the grade: it needs every individual check to be known and passing at the moment it buys. See [Meme Hunter](/agents/meme-hunter).

<Callout tone="danger">
  New tokens are extremely risky. A clean grade today can't stop the liquidity being pulled tomorrow.
</Callout>

## Buy by hand or with an agent

Trade a token from its row, or set up a Meme Hunter agent to watch for new launches that meet your rules. Meme trades go through RHEA and are public on NEAR.
