DCA (dollar-cost averaging) buys a fixed amount of a token on a schedule, so you build a position over time instead of all at once.

```text
Buy 5 NEAR worth of USDC every 12 hours for two weeks.
```

## What it needs

- The token to buy and the token to pay with.
- The amount per buy, and a minimum below which a buy is skipped.
- The schedule and its time zone, with an end date or a number of buys.
- A total cap, plus fee and slippage limits.

## How it runs

Each scheduled time is one slot. When a slot comes due, the agent gets a fresh quote, the Guard checks it, and the agent buys once.

- **One buy per slot.** A restart or a duplicate check never buys the same slot twice.
- **No catch-up.** If the agent was paused or a slot was missed, it doesn't buy the missed slots all at once. You review this before starting.
- **Time zones are kept.** Slots follow the time zone you chose, including daylight-saving changes.

The agent pauses itself if the budget runs out, the route isn't available or the quote is stale.

<Callout tone="tip">
  A buy that's in flight when you pause still settles. Its result shows in Activity.
</Callout>
