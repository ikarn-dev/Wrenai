Wrenai has no passwords or email accounts. You sign in with your NEAR wallet, and your NEAR account is your Wrenai account.

## How sign-in works

When you choose **Connect NEAR wallet**, the app opens NEAR Connect, NEAR's wallet connector. You pick your wallet and sign a one-time message. The message proves you own the account; it is not a transaction, so it costs nothing and moves no funds.

The app checks the signature, then keeps you signed in on that device. **Sign out** in Settings ends the session on that device.

## What signing in does not allow

Signing in lets Wrenai read your public balances and show your account. It does not let Wrenai move funds. Every deposit or transfer from your wallet asks your wallet for its own approval.

Agents trade through a separate, revocable approval. See [Trading access](/security/trading-access).

## Supported networks

Wrenai runs on **NEAR mainnet**, where trades move real funds. Tokens from other chains can be used when there is a verified NEAR ecosystem route for them.

The same app also runs on **NEAR testnet**, with test tokens. Choose **Try as a guest** to try it without a wallet, or connect a testnet wallet. A testnet session is separate from your mainnet sign-in. See [Try testnet](/get-started/testnet).

<Callout tone="warning">
  If your wallet shows a sign-in request you didn't start, reject it. Wrenai only asks you to sign while you're using the app.
</Callout>
