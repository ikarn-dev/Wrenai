**Spot** is for swapping tokens by hand. Pick a market, enter an amount, review the quote and confirm it. No agent is created.

## Where a swap pays from

| Source | Route | Visibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Private balance | NEAR Intents | Confidential |
| Wallet | RHEA or NEAR Intents | Public on NEAR |

Swaps from your private balance only use tokens that no agent holds, so a manual trade never spends an agent's funds.

## Make a swap

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the market">
    The Spot table lists markets with live prices. Pick one to open the trade ticket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a quote">
    Enter the amount. The quote shows what you pay, the least you receive, the fees and a countdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Press **Confirm swap**. From the wallet, your wallet asks you to approve the transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The card follows the swap until it settles, then shows what you received and the transaction. If a quote expires, refresh it and review the new one.

<Callout tone="info">
  Swapping native NEAR from your wallet sends one NEAR transfer; you won't see a separate wrap to wNEAR first.
</Callout>

## Automate it

After a trade, **Automate with an agent** opens a Spot Trader setup with your pair filled in. It is reviewed and funded separately.
