This guide takes about five minutes. You need a NEAR wallet with some NEAR for gas, and the token you want an agent to trade.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Go to the Wrenai app and choose **Connect NEAR wallet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the message">
    Your wallet asks you to sign a short message that proves you own the account. Signing is free and moves no funds. See [Connect your wallet](/get-started/connect-wallet).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look around">
    **Portfolio** shows your balances, agents and recent activity. Everything in the app reads from your real account; nothing is sample data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ask Jarvis

Open **Chat** and ask a question, for example:

```text
Should I go long or short NEAR today?
```

Jarvis reads live market data and answers with a lean, its reasons and what would change its mind. Each reply is checked against the model's signature before it is saved. See [Ask and research](/jarvis/ask-and-research).

## Create your first agent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick an agent">
    Open **Agents** and choose a type, such as DCA.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it in plain words">
    For example: buy 5 NEAR of USDC every 12 hours for a week. Choose the token and the amount the agent may use from your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and save">
    Wrenai turns your words into exact rules and shows them for review. Saving reserves the budget and creates the agent, paused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add funds and start">
    Set up trading access once, approve the deposit in your wallet, then press **Start**. See [How agents work](/agents/how-agents-work).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout tone="tip">
  You can create and review agents before setting up trading access. Nothing trades until you fund and start an agent.
</Callout>

## Stop or get your funds back

- **Pause** or **Stop** an agent from its page. **Stop all** on the Agents page stops every agent at once.
- **Claim** sends an agent's funds back to your wallet. See [Claims](/agents/claims).
