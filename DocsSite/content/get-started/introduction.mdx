Wrenai is a NEAR app for researching markets and running trading agents. You ask **Jarvis** about a token, a yield or a setup, and get an answer from a verified private model. When you want something to keep happening, you turn the plan into an **agent**: a fixed set of rules with its own budget, which checks the market on a schedule and trades only inside the limits you reviewed.

Wrenai runs on NEAR mainnet, where every trade moves real funds and pays real fees. To try it first, [use testnet as a guest](/get-started/testnet): free test tokens, no wallet needed.

## How Wrenai keeps you in control

- **You approve the limits.** An agent can only spend the exact tokens you reserved for it, within the per-order, daily and total limits you reviewed.
- **The model never signs.** AI explains data and turns your words into rules you review. Deciding, sizing and signing a trade is plain code that the model cannot change.
- **Every trade passes the Guard.** Before anything is signed, the [Guard](/security/the-guard) checks the limits, a fresh quote, fees and slippage. If a check can't run, the trade doesn't run.
- **Stop is one click.** **Stop all** on the Agents page stops every agent at once, with no confirmation step.
- **You can see what it's doing.** Each agent's page shows its current step, its next order, what will stop it and its PnL.

## The seven agents

| Agent | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Private Payments | Sends tokens from your private balance to a recipient |
| DCA | Buys a fixed amount on a schedule |
| Spot Trader | Buys and sells a spot pair, with take-profit and stop-loss exits |
| Perps | Trades Hyperliquid perpetuals with stops held by the venue |
| Meme Hunter | Buys new NEAR listings only when every risk check passes |
| Buy the Dip | Buys when the price falls to a level or drawdown you set |
| Sell the Top | Sells tokens you own when your profit targets are reached |

<Callout tone="info">
  Agents don't predict the market. Each one follows the rules you reviewed, and a passed check is not a promise about what a price will do next.
</Callout>
