Buy the Dip buys a spot token when its price falls to a level you set, or by a percentage from a recent high.

```text
Buy 10 USDC of NEAR each time it drops 8% from its 24-hour high, up to three times.
```

## What it needs

- The token, the token you pay with, and the chain.
- The trigger: an exact price, or a drawdown from a high over a window you define.
- How long the price must hold before buying (confirmation).
- The amount per buy, the most buys and the total budget.
- A cooldown, and when the agent may arm again.
- Fee and slippage limits, and an end date.

## How it runs

The agent tracks the high and its own state, so a restart picks up where it left off. Prices come from the venue the agent trades on, so a token on NEAR is never triggered by an unrelated exchange price.

- **One buy per dip.** Each dip buys once, then waits for your re-arm rule.
- **No chasing.** If price data has a gap, the agent waits for fresh data rather than reconstructing the move or buying an old dip.
- **Fresh quote.** Each buy gets a new quote and passes the Guard.

Buy the Dip only buys spot tokens; it doesn't use leverage.
