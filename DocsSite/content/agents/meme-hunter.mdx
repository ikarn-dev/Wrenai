Meme Hunter looks for genuinely new NEAR token launches and buys only those that pass every risk check you set.

```text
Buy 2 NEAR of new Nearly launches under 30 minutes old with at least 5,000 USDC of liquidity.
```

## Where it looks

New listings come from Squint's index of NEAR launchpads, including Nearly and Meme.Cooking. A listing only counts when its creation is confirmed on NEAR, and it must be younger than the age you set. Profiles, boosts and metadata changes are not launches.

## The checks

A candidate is skipped unless **every** check has a known, passing result at that moment:

- The token contract is a known, unmodified type, with no mint, freeze, admin or upgrade powers.
- Supply, decimals and holder and creator concentration are within your limits.
- The pool's ownership and reserves are known, and its liquidity meets your minimum.
- There are no transfer taxes.
- A buy quote and a sell quote both work at your size, with price impact inside your limit.

Right now Meme Hunter trades Nearly launches with locked pools on RHEA, paired with wNEAR. Other launch types are skipped.

<Callout tone="danger">
  Passing every check is not a safety guarantee. A token can still lose its liquidity or become hard to sell later. Only use what you can afford to lose.
</Callout>

## How it trades

Buys and sells go through RHEA from your trading account and are public on NEAR. Exits follow your take-profit, stop-loss and age rules. A failed check on a token you hold never blocks selling it.
