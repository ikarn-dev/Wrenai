Private Payments sends supported tokens from your private balance to a recipient, once or on a schedule.

```text
Pay alice.near 25 USDC on the first of every month until June.
```

## What it needs

- The exact recipient, token and amount.
- When to pay: once, or a schedule with an end date.
- Fee and slippage limits.
- Where the payment lands: confidential by default, or an explicitly chosen public NEAR wallet.

## Private or public delivery

| Delivery | What the recipient gets | Who can see it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confidential (default) | Funds in their private NEAR Intents balance | Kept confidential inside NEAR Intents |
| Public NEAR wallet | Tokens in their NEAR account | The payout transfer is public on NEAR |

You choose public delivery explicitly; an agent never switches a private payment to public on its own. For a public payout, Wrenai checks the account exists and is registered for the token. It never pays storage for the recipient.

## How it runs

Each payment gets a fresh quote and passes the Guard. A payment counts as sent only when the provider reports final delivery; a full refund comes back to your private balance.

<Callout tone="warning">
  Check the recipient carefully. A payment that has been delivered can't be reversed.
</Callout>

For one-off sends from chat, see [Payments](/jarvis/payments).
